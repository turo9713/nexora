# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Nexora a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, protect private
information, and prioritize user safety. Harassment, threats, discrimination,
doxxing, credential disclosure, and abuse of project infrastructure are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may remove
content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary. Reports
will be handled confidentially and consistently with the Contributor Covenant
2.1 enforcement guidelines.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
